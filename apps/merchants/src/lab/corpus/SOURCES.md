# Lab corpus: sources and public-domain basis

The `index.ts` in this folder is the whole corpus behind `lab-extract-schemabreak` and `lab-extract-drift` (CZ-20 §5.8a, PM-041 ruling of 2026-11-05).

## Rules this corpus follows

- Every text is a short excerpt of a few sentences, never a whole work.
- Every text was first published before 1929, so it is in the public domain in the United States on its date alone. Where a text is also a document of the United States government, the row says so; the date is still the basis.
- A text whose status was in any doubt was left out.
- Nothing is fetched at run time. The URL column is the identifier a buyer sends and the public page the excerpt was taken from; the lab never requests it, and the URLs were not re-checked live when this file was written (no page was fetched while building it).
- The excerpts were transcribed by the builder and may differ in punctuation or spelling from any one edition; they are not a substitute for the published works. The text is data: it is returned as-is, labelled `untrusted`, and never acted on.
