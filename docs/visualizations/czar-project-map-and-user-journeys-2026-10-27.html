Flow 4 · Evidence becomes discovery

Flows 2 and 3 end with a paid result. Flow 4 is what that payment teaches Czar. 4A turns every paid call into one gated evidence unit, adds the paying person’s own verdict and produces the ranking the planner uses in Flows 2 and 3 and the approval route in Flow 1. 4B lets each person’s representative share those outcomes as structured cards and returns three bounded signals. 4B reads 4A; it never writes it.

The loopFlow 2/3 payment → 4A evidence unit → payment gate → verdict → ranking → Flow 1 approval route and Flow 2/3 supplier choice → next payment.
Where 4B sitsBeside 4A, not inside it: incidents reorder and force approval, lessons swap near-ties, leads add unverified candidates. Scores never change.
What exists todayReceipts with indexer-checked settlement, acceptance profiles, the OpenEvidenceLog contract and an interim selector. Everything else in Flow 4 is designed (I-F-6, I-F-7), not built.

4A · Selection Layer evidence

Status: the interim selector (SCR-131) returns ranked = [] for every query, so today every x402 purchase is an exploration-slot plan that needs the passkey, and nothing is learned from it. 4B · Agent social-network evidence

Status: nothing is built (I-F-7, iteration 1). It reads Flow 4A’s tables, so it has nothing to say until 4A exists, and its incident and lesson routes act on ranked[], which is empty under the interim selector. With one real owner per local server, incidents and cross-owner lessons are demonstrable only with controlled representatives.
