Design basis and current limits

These are interface proposals for everyday professionals who use Czar to complete work, plus the people who inspect supplier decisions. All services, representatives, counts, timestamps, and prices are fictional controlled examples. The 30 Oct Flow 4 decisions supersede older fill-rate, latency, and inferred-verdict language in the 27 Oct project map and I-F-6/I-F-7 drafts. The outcome-based ranking formula and rewritten specifications are still pending, so candidate order here demonstrates UI behavior rather than a validated calculation.
